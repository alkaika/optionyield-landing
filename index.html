About

What is OptionYield?

OptionYield is a mobile options income app for retail investors who write covered calls and cash-secured puts. Instead of paging through slow chain viewers, you get a purpose-built covered call screener and cash-secured put scanner that filter the live options market by minimum annualized yield and out-of-the-money buffer — the two numbers that decide whether a trade is worth writing.

Every contract comes with a transparent breakdown: static return, annualized yield, downside protection and max profit, computed from real-time quotes with the formula shown inline. A reorderable watchlist, saved scans and honest “ESTIMATED” labeling on hybrid quotes round out a tool built for running an options income strategy from your phone — with the math to back every number.

The math

Every number, out in the open

Most tools hide the formulas behind a paywall. We print them. Here is exactly how OptionYield computes the metrics on every contract.

Static return premium ÷ stockPrice × 100 The flat yield if assigned today.
Annualized yield staticReturn × (365 ÷ DTE) Makes 7-day and 45-day trades comparable.
Max profit % (capitalGain + premium) ÷ stockPrice × 100 Total upside including price appreciation to strike.
OTM % distance from spot to strike, by side Your downside protection buffer on a covered call.

What strategies does OptionYield support?

Covered calls and cash-secured puts — the two core income strategies. The scanner and dashboard are purpose-built around them, including per-side OTM% and the optional 30-day high/low boundary rule.

How fresh is the market data?

Spot prices and option quotes stream to the app over a realtime relay and refresh on roughly a 15-second heartbeat — no manual pull-to-refresh. When a quote is a hybrid estimate, it is explicitly labeled “ESTIMATED”.

How is annualized yield calculated?

Static return (premium ÷ stock price) is scaled by 365 ÷ days-to-expiry. That makes short-dated and long-dated contracts directly comparable. The full formula for every metric is shown in the app — nothing is hidden.

No. OptionYield is an informational and educational tool. All metrics are computed from market data to help you do your own analysis. Options trading involves substantial risk — please see the full disclaimer below.
